The Data
Garage

Every car in Forza Horizon 6 gets the same test day, from sunrise to lights out. Same driver, same rules, no tuning. As the day goes on, the grip goes up and the safety net comes off: stock tires and every assist in the morning, semi-slicks and fewer assists by sunset, and by nightfall it's race slicks with nothing to catch it. The goal is simple: a fair, repeatable way to see how every car really stacks up. Fresh results are rolling in.

08

Methodology

The fine print behind every test. Anyone with FH6 can replicate every number on this page. That's the point.

AI

Same Driver Every Time

Every lap is driven by ANNA at Unbeatable, so the times show the car, not the driver. Manual gearbox, launch control on, simulation steering, damage off, fall, dry, no traffic. Body changes, like a removed wing, are logged and apply to all three tests.

±.017

Timing Precision

Times are accurate to about 0.017s, one 60 Hz frame. Gaps smaller than that are a tie.

R

What's Left Out

R class is mostly purpose-built track cars, so it isn't ranked yet. Listed R cars are for reference.
