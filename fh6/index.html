06 / METHOD

How We Test

Anyone with FH6 can replicate every number on this page. That's the point.

01

Sunrise Sprint

Sunflower Charge route, Hokubu region. A solo standing-start sprint on the car's stock tires with every assist on, like the owner would drive it. Result: time. A sprint, so it isn't comparable to lap times.

02

Golden Hour

Hokubu Circuit at sunset. Five solo laps on stock tires with ABS on and traction and stability off, like a sport mode. The first laps warm the tires. Result: fastest lap.

03

7 Laps in Heaven

Legend Island Circuit at night. The final test: semi-slicks go on and every assist comes off for a solo seven-lap race. Result: 7-lap total.

AI

Same Driver Every Time

Every lap is driven by ANNA at Unbeatable, so the times show the car, not the driver. Manual gearbox, launch control on, simulation steering, damage off, fall, dry, no traffic. Body changes, like a removed wing, are logged and apply to all three tests.

±.017

Timing Precision

Times are accurate to about 0.017s, one 60 Hz frame. Gaps smaller than that are a tie.
