06 / METHOD

How We Test

Anyone with FH6 can replicate every number on this page. That's the point.

01

Sunflower Sunrise Sprint

Sunrise. A solo point-to-point sprint on the car's stock tires, standing start, no traffic. A sprint, so its times aren't comparable to lap times.

02

Hokubu × 5

Sunset. A solo five-lap private event on stock tires. The first laps warm the tires; the fastest lap is the result. Body panel changes are allowed and logged.

03

7 Laps in Heaven

Night. The final test: semi-slicks go on for a solo seven-lap race around Legend Island. The 7-lap total is the result.

AI

Same Driver Every Time

Every lap is driven by ANNA at Unbeatable, so the times show the car, not the driver. Manual gearbox, launch control on, simulation steering, damage off, fall, dry, no traffic. ABS, traction and stability settings are being finalized.

±.017

Timing Precision

Times are accurate to about 0.017s, one 60 Hz frame. Gaps smaller than that are a tie.

R

What's Left Out

R class is mostly purpose-built track cars, so it isn't ranked yet. Listed R cars are for reference.
